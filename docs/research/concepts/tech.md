## borrow
- Let visitors pick their own entry point by situation rather than reading a list of services. Maxima Therapy (https://maximatherapy.com/), Awwwards Site of the Day on 7 Apr 2026 (Louis Paquet, KOKI-KIKO), routes visitors by life stage (0–3 / 3–18 / 18–65 / 65+). Its affirming plain-language tagline and its own illustration system replace stock photos. A psychologist site could open with doors like «тревога», «отношения», «выгорание», «для родителей».
- Use one committed brand palette instead of generic pastel. Maxima Therapy uses only two colours (#FDCB40 and #FD4401). Institute of Health (https://www.instituteofhealth.com), Awwwards Honorable Mention on 5 Feb 2026, uses only off-white #F8F4ED and charcoal #242424 with type-led fullscreen sections. Lesson: restraint plus one owned accent. Derive that accent from the practitioner's own photography (the project has tulips, sunset and beach shots) rather than a template palette.
- Use playful but purposeful page transitions and a designed 404 page (Maxima Therapy). A static site can do this natively with cross-document @view-transition (Chrome and Safari, with no cost in Firefox) instead of React or GSAP. Keep it to one shared element, such as the portrait or the section title, morphing between pages.
- Show the actual tool, not a description of it. Bevel Health (https://www.bevel.health/), Awwwards Honorable Mention on 11 May 2026 (NOOON), puts app-style UI fragments and data visualisation in the hero. For a therapy site: embed the live breathing timer or grounding exercise right in the first screen, usable without signing up.
- Write explainer content backed by evidence, in calm long-form reading. Harvard Health's Mindscape (https://www.health.harvard.edu/mindscape), a 2026 Webby Honoree in Health & Wellness (Rare Bird + Harvard Health Publishing), pairs custom illustration and video with sourced mental-health content. Borrow: short articles with a 60–70ch measure, 18–20px body text, text-wrap: pretty, and a credentials or sources line.
- Pace rest-focused storytelling over a long scroll. Eight Sleep (https://www.eightsleep.com/, site by Red Antler) was a 2026 Webby Honoree in Health & Wellness. Its specific motion and design details were not verified, so review it visually before borrowing.
- For a practitioner-scale reference, RewireMe (https://rewireme.com.au/), a Sydney psychologist site by Studio la Tas, is featured in Awwwards Inspiration for its video element. This is a feature, not an award. It shows how one calm full-bleed video can carry the hero of a solo-practice site.
- One orchestrated motion moment instead of motion everywhere. Put the motion into the breathing exercise and page transitions, use static reading sections, and keep everything gated on prefers-reduced-motion. Sound and vibration are off by default, and every tool works with motion and sound disabled.
- Make dark mode a real 'evening' theme: a separately tuned palette via light-dark() and color-scheme, not an inverted copy. Declare <meta name=color-scheme> so Samsung Internet doesn't force-darken it.
- Use plain, specific CTAs that say what happens next, for example «Записаться на первую встречу — 50 минут, онлайн или в кабинете» instead of «Начать путь». Make crisis and hotline information easy to find and visually quiet.
- Use real photography of the practitioner and space (irina-portrait, irina-window) at generous size, with honest captions. This builds trust better than illustrations of abstract heads and plants.
## avoid
- Purple or indigo-to-blue gradients, gradient text in headlines, and gradient CTAs with no meaning (traced to Tailwind's old bg-indigo-500 default).
- Inter or Roboto or the system sans everywhere, with no deliberate type scale or pairing.
- The stock page order: hero → logo or credential badges → 3 icon cards → pricing → testimonials carousel → FAQ, in that sequence, regardless of content.
- A uniform card kit: identical rounded-2xl cards (~16px radius), the same shadow, the same padding and equal heights, so there is no hierarchy.
- A thick coloured border on one side of a rounded card, cited in 2026 as the single most recognisable AI-UI signature.
- Reflexive glassmorphism or backdrop-blur panels, used where depth has no meaning.
- The same fade-up-on-scroll animation on every section, and buttons that snap instead of easing.
- Filler or hedging copy: «Elevate», «Unlock your potential», «your journey to wellness», «holistic approach», «may help», «best-in-class», and unsourced metrics like «500+ счастливых клиентов».
- The 'second wave' defaults: warm-editorial cream #F4F1EA with terracotta #D97757, near-black with an acid-green or vermilion accent, and broadsheet cosplay (hairlines, zero radius, dense columns) on a site with no editorial content.
- Template chrome: tracked all-caps eyebrow labels above every heading, «01 / 02 / 03» numbering, A·B·C meta lines, and pill 'Live' or 'New' badges.
- Stock or AI imagery: diverse people smiling at laptops, overly smooth 3D blobs, and AI-rendered serene women with closed eyes.
- (Own judgement for the wellness niche, not from the sources) A default sage-green and beige palette with lotus, leaf or brain-with-heart line icons in pastel circles, and an emoji or Lucide icon above every card.
- (Own judgement) Repeated «Бесплатная консультация» buttons in every section, and testimonial sliders with initial-letter avatars and five gold stars.
